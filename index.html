Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI image generator work?
Simply upload a high-quality photo of your product, and our deep learning model parses the product geometry, placing it realistically inside studio settings with custom lighting and reflections.
Can I export generated images for commercial use?
Yes! All generations created inside the AI Marketing Studio on Pro and Enterprise tiers are 100% owned by you and cleared for worldwide commercial distribution.
How accurate is the product details parsing?
Our models preserve all textual labels, brand logos, and specific geometric features of your product while generating organic shadows and dynamic lighting around the bottle, device, or jar.
